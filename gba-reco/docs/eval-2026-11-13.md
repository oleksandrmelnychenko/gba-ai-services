# Measurements for v42, 2026-11-13

Model version `v42-confirmed-sales-history-floor-20250101` (co-purchase lists:
`copurchase-v5-confirmed-sales-history-floor-20250101`). DEV `ConcordDb_V5`, sales synced to
2026-11-13. The v41 decision record on the previous purchase definition is
`eval-2026-11-12.md`.

## What changed against v41

1. Purchase history follows gba-server's confirmed sale (`app/data/source_rules.py`):
   invoiced, non-deleted, not an accounting carrier, valid non-deleted line of positive
   quantity, dated by the invoice instant on Kyiv days, fully returned lines dropped. v41 used
   `Order.Created` and `IsValidForCurrentSale` only.
2. One card per article (sibling cards), with the in-stock sibling standing in for a bought
   card that has no stock.
3. Services and products in the client's open orders (last 30 days) are never listed.
4. A product bought under any agreement of the client is labelled a repeat purchase, never new.
5. Stock on storages marked not for sale never counts, also for lists without an agreement.

## Latency, 50 clients, sequential, trial instance

| | /recommend p50 / p95 | /recommend/copurchase p50 / p95 |
|---|---|---|
| warm (snapshot payload in Redis) | 6 ms / 21 ms | 6 ms / 6 ms |
| cold, v42 | 4.3 s / 6.1 s | 2.2 s / 3.6 s |
| cold, v41 | 2.6 s / 4.5 s | 2.1 s / 3.3 s |
| cold-start client (no history), v42 | 1.0 to 1.9 s | |
| seeded cross-sell (one cart product), v42 | | 2.0 s (max 3.0 s) |

Cold is the per-request SQL path: contexts outside the nightly snapshot, a non-default
`top_n`, region scope, or seeds. The confirmed-sale rule joins `Sale` and anti-joins carriers
and fully returned lines in every query, and the request reads open orders and client-wide
ownership; that costs about 1.7 s on `/recommend`. The first form of the rule (a correlated
return sum) measured 4.3 s / 10.2 s and 5.9 s / 9.5 s and was replaced.

## Snapshot memory and time

One refresh process: 264 MB after loading the rows, 345 MB after building the snapshot, 382 MB
peak; the two dense client × product matrices (1,039 × 15,780, float32) are 131 MB of that.
Load 9 s, 1,879 contexts in 79 s on four threads. The API process holds no snapshot.

## Cold-start list

A client without confirmed purchases gets 25 «Популярний товар» items: the most sold lines
since 2025-01-01 after the exclusions above, in stock in that context's storages. It is the
same list for every such client with the same stock buckets and identical on repeat.
