# Offline evaluation, 2026-11-12

Decision record for model version `v41-group-cap12-history-floor-20250101`.

## Protocol

- Temporal holdout on the snapshot engine (`app/services/eval/holdout.py`).
- Six origins: 2026-05-13, 06-13, 07-13, 08-13, 09-13, 10-13. The snapshot uses only
  history strictly before the origin; truth is what each active client bought in the
  following 30 days (valid sale lines, quantity net of returns, ubiquitous lines excluded).
- 400 to 435 clients per origin had purchases in the window (2,515 cases in total).
- The served list is the default 25 items. `@10` metrics score the first 10 of that list.
- `new_hit@25`: share of clients who bought a product new to them and had one in the list.
- `coverage@25`: distinct products recommended across all cases.
- `revenue_hit@25`: EUR of window purchases that were in the list, divided by EUR purchased.
- Stock is today's, not point-in-time: `ProductAvailability` holds current balances only and
  this pass did not rebuild history from consignment movements. Every variant sees the same stock.
- Selection used the first four origins; the last two were held back for confirmation.
- Cells are mean ± population standard deviation across origins.

## Reproduce

```
.venv/bin/python -m app.services.eval.holdout \
  --origins 2026-05-13,2026-06-13,2026-07-13,2026-08-13,2026-09-13,2026-10-13
```

`--variants '{"name": {"max_per_group": 5}}'` evaluates custom parameter sets
(`repurchase_count`, `max_per_group`, `halflife_days`, `discovery_source`, `popular_scope`,
`seasonal_boost`). `--write-measured` stores the first variant's precision in
`app/services/eval/measured.json`, which the service reports as `precision_estimate`.

## Decision

Ship rule: win on the confirmation origins on `hit@25` and `revenue_hit@25` without losing
more than 5% coverage.

- **Group cap 3 → 12: shipped as v41.** On the confirmation origins `hit@25` rises from 0.529
  to 0.549 and `revenue_hit@25` from 0.096 to 0.118, with coverage up 2.6%. Removing the cap
  entirely measures the same `hit@25` and slightly higher revenue (0.124); 12 keeps a guard
  against a list made of one product group. The cost is fewer co-purchase backfill slots, so
  `new_hit@25` falls from 0.061 to 0.050.
- **Dropping similar-client discovery: not shipped.** It raises `hit@25` by about 0.006 to
  0.009 and `new_hit@25` from 0.061 to 0.094, but loses 8% to 10% coverage in every
  combination, which fails the ship rule. `DISCOVERY_SOURCE=copurchase` switches it on.
- **Half-life 14 days:** +0.003 to +0.004 `hit@25` on top of the cap change, inside the
  spread between origins. Not shipped.
- **25/0 split:** best `hit@25` and precision, but `new_hit@25` halves. Not shipped.
- **15/10 split, popularity by own groups, seasonal boost:** no gain or a loss on `hit@25`.
